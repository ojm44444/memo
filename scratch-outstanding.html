Backup / iCloud section

iCloud is not a backup. It is a mirror.

"Delete a memo on your phone to free up space and it goes from everywhere at once. That is not a bug, it is what syncing means, and it is how most people find out. There is no undo and no copy left behind."

A synced folder — "One library, reflected on every device. Remove it once and it is removed."
songdrafts — "Your recordings are stored on your device, not streamed from somewhere else. Deleting here does not delete it everywhere: deleted songs wait 30 days in the trash, and you can put them back. And you can pull the whole library out as a zip whenever you want. That is the difference between a copy and a mirror."

FAQ — now two‑column, heading left / list right

Does it work without Wi‑Fi?

Yes, properly. Not a cut‑down offline mode.

What happens to a song I never finish?

It sits exactly where you left it. Forever, if that's how it goes.

Can I share demos with my producer or bandmates?

Send them a listen link. They never have to make an account.

Does it work on my phone?

Import via the Files app on iPhone, run it in your mobile browser, install it to your home screen. There is no App Store app.

Is my music private?

On your device first, a copy syncs to storage so it reaches your other devices. Nobody reads it, nobody trains anything on it.

What if I stop paying?

songdrafts locks, your music doesn't. Download everything in one zip any time, including after you stop.
